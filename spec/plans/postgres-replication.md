Confirmed production defect: `/tmp/xeip-postgres-replication-red.log` reaches all eight source states and then fails because cancelled synchronous COMMIT returns a successful proposal. PostgreSQL18.6 [`syncrep.c`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/postgres/postgres/REL_18_6/src/backend/replication/syncrep.c) emits a WARNING after a locally committed transaction loses its requested remote wait; the driver delivers it as a notice, not a rejected query.

Repair design: explicitly request warning messages, reject any notice during COMMIT without exposing its text, and discard that connection. Emit an empty transactional `pg_catalog.pg_logical_emit_message(true, 'xeip.durability', '', false)` before the final authorization guard for every transaction.
