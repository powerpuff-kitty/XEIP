## Review focus

- A policy/binding update during a remote authentication or final effect check must invalidate the old request, including updates that restore the same configuration.
- Reopen and update must never reset authority history or expose credentials; configuration-event corruption must fail closed.
- Retired provider instances must release bearer tokens and cannot revalidate cloned, released or previous-instance principals.
- Old executor policy captures must not authorize an effect after current policy or approver binding changes.
- Schema upgrade must preserve existing consumed claims, signed trust and all action audit rows; deployment claims must distinguish real provider tests from synthetic endpoints.
