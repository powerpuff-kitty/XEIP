## Review focus

- Authentication/body deadlines must prevent deferred unauthenticated journal calls and retained concurrent buffers.
- Redirect/error/length/UTF8 ambiguity must never leak credentials or produce an acknowledgement.
- Successful replies must bind exact scope/cursor/predecessor and preserve fail-closed source reconciliation on ambiguous writes.
- Full16 MiB journal payload must be testable without silent transport truncation; claims about hosted memory/CPU stay unproven.
- A configured handler must not add namespace initialization/reset or expose owner authority; deployment wiring remains explicit separate work.
